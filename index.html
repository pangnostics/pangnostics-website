Pangnostics’ PanViewTM digital flow cytometer (dFC) employs planar microfluidics to enable 100% single-fluorophore detection at high particle throughput, enabling greater sensitivity and capabilities than conventional macrofluidic flow cytometers.

THE PARADIGM SHIFT

Turning flow cytometry upside down.

Conventional nanoscale flow cytometers use a top-down approach in which technologies developed for cells were adapted for nanoparticles.Pangnostics takes a bottom-up approach that starts with single-molecule detection (SMD).

CONVENTIONAL FLOW CYTOMETRY

  • Macrofluidic sheath-flow systems
  • Insufficient sensitivity to detect every fluorophore
  • Cannot detect single molecules or small EVs
  • Quantitation requires external calibration

PANGNOSTICS dFC

  • Planar microfluidic system
  • 100% single-fluorophore sensitivity
  • Detects every fluorescent biomolecule or particle
  • Absolute quantitation via single-molecule counting

HOW dFC WORKS

Making every molecule count

Explore how dFC detects every fluorescent molecule.

Watch how individual counts become a distribution.

Because fluorophore emission is stochastic, the detected photon bursts can vary widely from one fluorophore to another. The high sensitivity of dFC ensures the detection of even the dimmest fluorophore population.

SINGLE-PARTICLE ANALYSIS

Phenotyping individual EVs

With 4 excitation lasers and 12 color channels, dFC can unmask the biological heterogeneity of biomedical nanoparticles such as EVs in a single experiment—down to the smallest nanoparticle.

SINGLE-PARTICLE ANALYSIS

Absolute concentration measurements

Direct counting of fluorescent antibodies or EV subpopulations

By detecting every molecule that flows through the channel, dFC achieves absolute quantitation of the concentration of fluorescently-labeled biomolecules or EV subpopulations.

SINGLE-PARTICLE ANALYSIS

Absolute quantitation of biomarkers

The ultra-high sensitivity of dFC enables single-molecule counting of protein and nucleic acid biomarker copy numbers associated with each EV—without external calibration.
